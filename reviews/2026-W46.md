## M1 / M2 / W1 — due, still not run (why)

Same two blockers as the last two weeks; they need your decision, not a quiet change by me:

1. **Gap exclusion vs reconnect logging:** the pre-registered rule drops a coin if any recorder gap falls inside its
   window; the recorder logs every websocket reconnect as a gap (hundreds per run on bad runners), so most coins would
   be dropped → likely "NO EVIDENCE" for a data reason, and the one-time holdout would be spent.
2. **Size:** the loader loads all ~19 GB of recordings at once; a GitHub runner (~14 GB free disk, 16 GB RAM) would
   most likely crash.

No outcome (dead rates, P&L, wallet results) was computed or looked at. Only data-quality fields were read.
