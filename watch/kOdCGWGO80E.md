### 4. Contradictions & Security Red Flags

*   **The "Future" Timeline**: At **09:16**, the narrator's browser shows a YouTube video upload date of **Jan 3, 2026**. However, the portfolio screenshots shown at **03:11** are labeled "REAL PHOTOS OF MY PORT DEC 2024". If the video was recorded/uploaded in early 2026, referencing a portfolio from late 2024 as the primary proof of recent success is outdated. Alternatively, the browser dates may have been manipulated.
*   **Phishing Risk**: At **06:59**, the narrator instructs users to go to `phantom.com` to download the wallet. The official, legitimate website for the Phantom wallet is **`phantom.app`**. While the video shows him downloading the correct extension, telling beginners to type `.com` for crypto wallets is a dangerous practice that frequently leads to malicious phishing sites.
*   **Severe Security Contradiction**: At **10:18**, the narrator instructs users to export the **private key** of their main Phantom hot wallet and paste it directly into the Padre web terminal. 
    *   *The Contradiction*: Earlier in the video (08:15), he warns: *"Make sure you never show anybody this information... you are going to get fried and drained."* 
    *   Pasting a private key into a web-based trading terminal exposes the user to massive counterparty and smart contract risks. For a "beginner-friendly" course, recommending that users import their primary private key into a third-party website instead of using a dedicated, low-balance "burner" wallet is a major security red flag.
